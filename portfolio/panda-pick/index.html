PandaPick

PandaPick is a friendly office prediction game for teams — the risk-free way to turn watercooler chatter into a company-wide sport. An admin spins up a company workspace, invites the team by e-mail or a single share link, and everyone makes fun picks on challenges — light-hearted predictions with as many outcomes as you like. Lock in one pick before the deadline, earn +1 point when you're right, and climb a live leaderboard that crowns your team's reigning champion. 🐼👑

Alongside the picks are two daily mini-games, both scored into the same leaderboard. Mathdown is a 1v1 real-time maths showdown: challenge an online teammate and you both receive the same ten questions of ramping difficulty, one at a time, in sync — the first correct answer claims the point and advances you both. Most points wins; ties break on the lower total solve time. Pinpoint is a per-workspace treasure hunt: each day the workspace gets one board with a hidden treasure, members drop and drag a flag live, then lock it; at the workspace's deadline the board closes, the treasure is revealed with an animation, and the closest locked flags score.

Both games are built so they cannot be cheated from the client. Mathdown's answers live in a quiz_solutions table with RLS enabled and zero policies, so no client can ever read them — answers are checked only inside a security-definer function, and solve times are measured server-side. Pinpoint's treasure coordinates sit in the same kind of unreadable table and are only written into the public row at reveal time.

The twist on the product side is multi-workspace by design: one account belongs to as many teams as invite you, and you switch between them in a single click. Plan limits only ever cap the workspaces you create — joining a teammate's workspace is always free and unlimited, and the limits that do apply (weekly picks, member count) are taken from the workspace owner's plan and enforced by database triggers.

Under the hood it is a real multi-tenant SaaS: workspaces with role-based access, invitation-only membership, custom colour-coded categories, private / public and anonymous challenges, and every business rule enforced in Postgres Row Level Security and ten Supabase Edge Functions — from one-vote-per-pick to automatic point reversal when a result is re-resolved. It ships its own 6-digit OTP e-mail flow, seven responsive HTML e-mail templates, a searchable admin audit trail, in-app and e-mail notifications, per-company branding, dark mode and EN/FR.

Not a gambling product — no real money, no odds, no payouts. Just team morale, 100% HR-compliant by design.

Play & compete
  • Challenges with unlimited outcomes and a voting deadline
  • One-tap voting — one pick per member, locked at close
  • Change your pick any time before close, if the challenge allows it
  • Creators can be blocked from voting on their own challenge
  • Custom point values per challenge
  • Comments on challenges, switchable per challenge
  • Anonymous voting mode
  • Private and public challenges
  • Colour-coded custom categories
  • Live leaderboard with a reigning champion
  • My votes, pick reviews and a pending-resolutions queue

Mini-games
  • Mathdown — 1v1 real-time maths duel over ten synced questions
  • Server-generated questions with ramping difficulty and integer answers
  • First correct answer claims the point; skips and forfeits handled
  • Time-weighted scoring, with ties broken on total solve time
  • Daily Mathdown mode, with weekend scheduling rules
  • Match history and a lobby of online teammates
  • Pinpoint — one daily treasure board per workspace
  • Drop a flag, drag it live, then lock it before the deadline
  • Closest locked flags score (top-two distance groups, ties share)
  • Animated reveal ceremony, animal flag picker, per-workspace deadline
  • Pinpoint history, and both games toggleable per workspace

Workspaces & administration
  • Multi-workspace membership with a one-click switcher
  • Invitation-only joining, by e-mail or a single share link
  • Invitation management — send, resend, revoke
  • Role-based access, with a guarantee of at least one active admin
  • Member deactivation that immediately blocks voting
  • Ownership transfer
  • Company settings and per-company branding with logo upload
  • Searchable activity / audit log
  • Plan tiers with weekly pick and member limits, enforced in Postgres
  • Workspace usage reporting

Platform & correctness
  • Every rule enforced in Row Level Security, not only in the UI
  • Ten Deno Edge Functions — OTP, invitations, resolution, leaderboard, mail
  • Automatic point reversal and re-award when a result is re-resolved
  • Scheduled closing of expired challenges via a cron-secret endpoint
  • Own 6-digit OTP signup flow, plus native password reset
  • Seven responsive HTML e-mail templates
  • In-app notification bell and e-mail notifications
  • Realtime updates, dark mode and EN/FR
