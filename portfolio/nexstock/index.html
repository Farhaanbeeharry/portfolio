NexStock

NexStock is a business operating system for small and medium businesses — one platform in place of the usual pile of disconnected tools. It brings multi-store inventory, a point of sale, quotations and invoicing, customers and suppliers, reporting and audit logs into a single system, configurable enough to fit a retail shop, a wholesaler, a workshop, a garage or a repair business.

Architecturally it is a modular monolith: a Laravel 12 API where every bounded context — Inventory, Products, Sales, Purchasing, Customers, Suppliers, Accounting, Reporting, Users, Notifications, Settings, Support — is its own module with its own domain, application, infrastructure and routes layers, and a dependency linter that fails the build when a module reaches into a neighbour it has no business knowing about. The composition root wires modules together; it contains no business logic of its own.

On top of that API sit two clients: a React + TypeScript SPA for the back office and the POS, and a Flutter companion app for the shop floor — stock lookups, movements and dashboards from a phone. Both are fully bilingual (EN / FR) with light and dark themes, and the theme and language follow the account rather than the device.

Inventory & stock
  • Product catalogue with variants, barcodes and unit pricing
  • Per-store stock levels with low-stock thresholds
  • Stock adjustments with reason codes and a full audit trail
  • Inter-store transfers with request / dispatch / receive states
  • Bulk import & export of products
  • Barcode scanner input on product and POS screens

Selling
  • Touch-friendly POS with keyboard shortcuts and held orders
  • Product customisation at the point of sale (custom-order businesses)
  • Quotations that convert into invoices
  • Invoices with status transitions, part payments and follow-ups
  • Printable / thermal receipts and an orders queue for workshops
  • Accounts receivable with mark-as-paid and reminder flows

Business & platform
  • Multi-business workspaces with a business switcher
  • Guided onboarding wizard with logo upload & cropping
  • Users, roles and granular permissions
  • Customers and suppliers with per-contact history
  • Supplier orders and supplier product catalogues
  • Drag-and-drop customisable dashboard widgets
  • Reports with charts, plus notifications and a searchable audit log
  • JWT auth, 2FA (TOTP with QR enrolment), OTP e-mail flows
  • Documented REST API with generated OpenAPI spec & integrations page
