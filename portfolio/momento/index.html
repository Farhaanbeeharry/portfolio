MoMento

April 28, 2026 Farhaan Beeharry
web app angular project management supabase kanban
MoMento is a collaborative project-management platform that turns meeting minutes into action. Teams document minutes of meeting (attendees, decisions, notes), spin action items straight out of them, and drive delivery through a full Jira-style workflow — kanban and list boards, sprints with capacity planning, story points, test-case management and hierarchical project documents. Built with Angular 20 (standalone components, signals) on a Supabase backend.

Key features:
  • Minutes of meeting (draft/publish/archive) with attendees & authorship
  • Action items with issue types (Epic/Story/Bug/Task), priority & status workflow
  • Kanban board (drag & drop) + list view
  • Sprint planning with capacity & goals
  • Advanced multi-field filtering & search
  • Project documents in hierarchical folders (markdown + images)
  • Test-case management with CSV/markdown import
  • Team & role management
  • Project financing tracking
